THE ONE EXPERIMENT (predeclare before touching 2024)
A. Build fold-safe X176 for train<=2022 -> 2023 and train<=2023 -> 2024. Every EB,
   frequency, season-form, TrackMan, role, median and IQR statistic uses the fit side only.
B. Fit the exact 3-seed MLP above and average raw probabilities. Use no validation-selected
   epoch. On 2023 only, fit one logit-affine calibration and one convex coefficient against
   the frozen current OOF; freeze both, then apply unchanged to 2024.
C. PASS only if both 2023 analytic gain and locked 2024 gain are >=8, 2024 early and late
   gains are positive, and pitcher-cluster bootstrap p025>0. Otherwise stop: no final fit,
   test read, ZIP, or submission.
D. If PASS, refit the unchanged 3 seeds through 2024, build all LUTs from official train /
   TrackMan, and add the frozen coefficient to the current clean package. Test processing
   must be row-local; require fake-server, singleton/permutation/split QA and <600 seconds.

RUNTIME / DEPLOYABILITY
- Public source documents TrackMan matching about 3 min, the X168 build about 6 min,
  and MLP about 15 sec/seed on its RTX4090 development machine
  (trackman_match2.py:21-23; build_v13.py:20-22; tune_dl.py:14-25).
- Even allowing a large slowdown, two 3-seed folds plus preprocessing fit inside 60 min;
  impose a hard 45-minute validation stop so QA time remains.
- The MLP has about 1.91M parameters/seed (~23 MB fp32 for 3 seeds). With the current
  ~12.4 MB package, size is far below the 500 MB limit. Elephant's heavier full ensemble
  was estimated at 409/600 server seconds and passed row-independence
  (sj_stdmlp.md:187-204); MLP-only still requires our own measured rehearsal.
